A word about how this game was made

AI did not make this game. Neither did I, alone.

The first thing the AI produced when I asked for this game looked like an Atari 2600 cartridge: blocky shapes and a croc you had to squint to see. What you just played took hundreds of hours of me pushing and correcting, adding a croc's swagger, a cane fire at dusk, a boomerang that flies true. And the AI turned each idea into working code and tested it, often the same day.

I brought the imagination: Wally, Kenny, the King, the Cairns I love. The AI brought the technical side I couldn't do alone. Neither of us could have made this on our own.

And yet we did.

If you think AI can make things like this without people, remember that Atari croc. It took a human with an imagination, and an AI to build what he imagined.

Made the old way, a game like this would have needed a studio team, a year or more, and a big budget.

    For the love of Cairns

    The Cairns Esplanade
    The city's waterfront, with its free swimming Lagoon and the tidal mudflats where migratory shorebirds feed, some flying in from as far as Siberia.
    The Great Barrier Reef and Green Island
    The largest coral reef system in the world, World Heritage listed. Green Island is a coral cay in the reef, a short boat ride from Cairns.
    The Daintree
    Part of the Wet Tropics of Queensland World Heritage Area and often described as one of the oldest rainforests on Earth. Here two World Heritage areas, rainforest and reef, meet at the sea.
    Mossman Gorge
    Crystal-clear river and rainforest in the Daintree National Park, in the traditional country of the Eastern Kuku Yalanji people.
    Kuranda and Barron Falls
    The village in the rainforest, reached by the historic Kuranda Scenic Railway (finished in 1891) and the Skyrail over the treetops. Barron Falls roars after the wet season rains.
    Saltwater crocodiles
    The largest living reptiles, at home in Far North Queensland's rivers and estuaries. Wally is a friendly one, but real crocs are wild: be croc wise.

    Chomp 3D is set in Far North Queensland. We acknowledge the Traditional Owners of the lands and waters it celebrates, including the Gimuy Walubara Yidinji and Yirrganydji peoples of Cairns, the Djabugay people of Kuranda and the Eastern Kuku Yalanji people of the Daintree and Mossman Gorge, and pay our respects to Elders past and present.

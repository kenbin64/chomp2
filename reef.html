The Great Barrier Reef

Off Green Island, Wally dives from the end of the jetty. Under water there's no food, only air: watch the air meter, swim up to breathe, or stop at a bubble vent. Follow the glowing guide fish through the coral maze to the coral cave, then find the old pirate chest deep inside. Taking it makes the giant clam at the cave mouth snap shut, and only a pearl will open it, so bring pearls from the Esplanade and collect more on the way. Mind the box jellyfish.

Thank you for testing

Beta

Chomp 3D: Wally's Cairns Adventure is in beta. You are among the first to play it, and what you find makes it better before everyone else arrives. It is free and open to everyone. Personal portfolio / skills demonstration: not a commercial product.

Found something? Tell Ken

Anything broken, confusing, too hard, too easy, or just an idea: every message is read. Say which device and browser you used, and which level you were in.

Send feedback or report a problem

You can also reach the form from the landing page, the title screen and any pause (Paws) in the game.

Updates come often

In the beta there will be many updates, sometimes several a day. When a new one is out, the game shows an Update ready card between runs: tap Update. Your game, outfits and points are kept.

The version you are playing is on the title screen and on the About page. Put it in your message when you report a problem.

Install it like an app

  • Android, or Chrome and Edge on a computer: on the first page, tap Install the app.
  • iPhone and iPad: in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Or just play in the browser: the link is under the buttons on the first page.

It plays best on a phone held sideways.

Things to look for

  1. Getting started: one tap on Play should put you in the game. Was anything in the way?
  2. The controls: the green orb (drag to go and turn, tap to jump on a phone; hover and click on a computer), the mouse (click the game, then hold left to go), the keys, or a game controller. Which felt best? What felt clumsy?
  3. Control response: does Wally react the moment you move? Is turning too fast or too slow, the orb too sensitive or not enough? Does a tap or click always jump?
  4. Cultural sensitivity: is everyone shown with respect, especially Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people, their Country and their stories, and the many other people of Cairns? If anything is wrong, or could be done better, please tell Ken: it will be changed.
  5. Each level: Croc Park, the cane fields, the Esplanade, the reef, Kuranda and the zip line, Mossman Gorge, the Daintree and the rescue. Where did you get stuck, lost, or bored?
  6. Different screens: a phone upright and sideways, a tablet, a computer. Did buttons cover the game anywhere?
  7. Points and leaderboards: does it make you want another go?
  8. The places: if you know Cairns, does anything look wrong or out of place?
  9. Clunkiness: anything awkward or sticky, or that takes more taps or moves than it should.
  10. Slow animation or movement: Wally, the camera or the world stuttering, lagging or running slowly. Say which device.
  11. Getting stuck: anywhere Wally stops dead against something you cannot see, falls through the ground, or cannot get out.
  12. Buttons and cards: any that do nothing, cover the game, or will not close.
  13. Saving: close the game and come back: are your outfits, hats, points and progress still there?
  14. Words: spelling mistakes, or anything that reads oddly.
  15. Sound: too loud, too quiet, or missing.

Report any bug you find, however small: say the device, the browser, the level, and what happened just before.

Known rough edges

  • On older or low-power phones it may run slowly; tell Ken which phone.
  • The music is a placeholder, to be replaced.
  • Some levels are still being tuned for difficulty and length.

Send feedback any time

Big or small, it all helps. Open the feedback form.

Your privacy

Your game saves on your own device. Nothing about you is sold or shared, and there are no ads or tracking. Leaderboards keep only the name you choose and your score. More about privacy.

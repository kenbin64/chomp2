How to play

Wally's Cairns Adventure: a crocodile, a sceptre, and a trip across Cairns and Far North Queensland.

🧭 The full guide: every level, talisman and screenshot

The first thing

Wally starts as an ordinary crocodile at Croc Park in Cairns. Find the sceptre at the bottom of the pond: follow the gold arrow over the water and dive down to it.

Then

  • Catch the food. The visitors throw it when Wally is close, but not too close, or they run off. Catching it in the air scores more.
  • Keep fed. Food fills the feed-o-meter. When it drops into the red, Kenny the Koala brings his jar: get to it.
  • Follow the arrow. The arrow at the top of the screen always points to the next thing to do.
  • Collect the talismans. Each level hides one for the King's sceptre. Complete a level and the next one opens.
  • Watch out for box jellyfish, flying foxes after the food, cane toads, and the other crocs.

On a phone or tablet

  • The diamond of buttons, bottom right: hold the top to go forward, left or right to turn. Rest your thumb between the top and a side button to go forward while turning.
  • The gold middle button: jump.
  • The blue bottom button (the arrow under the wave): dive under water, or duck on land to get under branches.
  • Slide your thumb from button to button without lifting it.
  • Drag anywhere else on the screen to turn the camera.
  • ☰, at the top, holds the map, Paws (pause) and the rest.
  • Best with the phone held sideways.

On a computer

  • W A S D or the arrow keys to move, Space to jump, Shift to duck or dive.
  • The mouse: click the game, then move the mouse to turn, hold the left button to go, click to jump, and hold the right button to look around. Esc gives the cursor back.
  • The green orb, bottom right: rest the cursor on it to steer, click it to jump.
  • P Paws · M sound · Tab the map (after the first level) · F use the item Wally carries.

With a game controller

  • Left stick to steer (up goes the way he faces), A to jump, B or a trigger to duck or dive, Y for the camera behind him, Start for Paws.

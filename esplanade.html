The Esplanade

Wally has the run of the Esplanade boardwalk. The picnic groups keep him fed. Collect mangoes while the pelicans and fruit bats try to steal them. The chest by the Lagoon holds the Big Golden Mango for the giant clam at the reef. And the charter boat at the end of the pier will take him out after a black marlin, for a fare of mangoes: let the marlin go, and it gives him the sun medallion. Then the skipper runs him out to the reef.

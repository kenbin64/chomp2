The Esplanade

Wally has the run of the Esplanade boardwalk: up and down it, and off the side for a swim in the ocean (watch for stingers). The picnic groups will throw lunch if he doesn't crowd them. Feed out every group (each one leaves him a pearl, and pearls carry on to the Great Barrier Reef) and fill the feed-o-meter, and the Lagoon pool at the end is his way on. In the sea he can dive under (watch for box jellyfish) and crawl back up onto the boardwalk.

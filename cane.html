The Cane Fields

Out of Croc Park at dusk, Wally hides in the sugar cane. The cane is thick, but he has the machete from the park gate: walk into the cane to cut a path. Cutting knocks down pieces of sugar cane, and some lie on the ground already. Eat them to keep your energy up, before the cane toads and fruit bats get them (and mind the toads: poison skin). Where the cane is burning you can't go, but the fire can't reach you. In the middle of the field is the fire talisman.

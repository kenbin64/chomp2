Mossman Gorge

The Swimming Holes

The Mossman River runs clear over granite through the rainforest. Work your way down it: swim the holes, slide down the smooth rock slicks from one hole to the next, and keep fed from the visitors on the rocks. The key lies on the bottom of one hole, and the chest with the opal, the earth talisman, on the bottom of the last.
